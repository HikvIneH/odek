# Code of Conduct

odek follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent. Many people here write in a
  second language.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Critique code and designs, never the person
  who wrote them.
- No harassment, insults, sexual language or imagery, or personal attacks, and
  no publishing anyone's private information without their permission.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent
the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, send a direct message to the maintainer,
[@hikvineh on X](https://x.com/hikvineh). Reports are kept private.

The maintainer may remove comments, lock threads, or block people from the
project for behaviour that breaks this code, following the Contributor
Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
